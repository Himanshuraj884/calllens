# Northwind CRM sales playbook (sample data for the pilot)

## Pricing
Starter is 15 dollars per seat per month, Growth is 39 dollars, Enterprise is custom. Annual billing gives 20 percent off. Discounts above 20 percent need manager approval.

## Handling the too expensive objection
Ask what they compare against. Anchor on time saved: reps spend about 5 hours a week on manual data entry, which Northwind automates. Offer a 30 day pilot before discussing discounts.

## Competitor: Brightlane
Brightlane is cheaper but has no call transcription and limited integrations. Emphasise native Slack and email sync, and the call summary feature.

## Security and compliance
Northwind is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and offers India and EU data residency on Enterprise.

## Onboarding
Typical onboarding takes 2 weeks. A customer success manager is assigned for Growth and Enterprise. Data import from spreadsheets is supported on all plans.

## Next steps after a demo
Send the recap email within 24 hours, share the pricing sheet, and schedule a follow-up call with the decision maker within a week.
